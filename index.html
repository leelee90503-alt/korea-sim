Getting Started

How It Works

Four straightforward steps from purchase to full connectivity in Korea.

1

Purchase Online

Select your plan (LTE or 5G) and duration on KoreaSimStore.com. Your voucher is issued immediately. For same-day pickup, book at least 2 hours in advance.

2

Land in Korea

Upon arrival, proceed to the LG U+ counter at your arrival airport — Incheon T1 or T2, Gimpo, or Busan Gimhae. Select counters operate 24 hours.

Present & Receive

Find the LG U+ counter at the airport. Present your passport and booking voucher. Request a SIM test before leaving the counter.

Insert & Connect

Insert the SIM — it auto-activates immediately. No stress, no complicated setup. Our staff handles everything for you right at the counter.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

? Can I use this SIM card for identity verification? −

Data eSIMs and voice-enabled data SIMs cannot be used for personal identity (real-name) verification. However, basic phone-number-based SMS verification may work in some cases. Data-only SIMs do not support identity verification or SMS verification of any kind.

? Is this SIM compatible with all phone models? +

The Korea SIM card is compatible with most unlocked (carrier-unlocked) smartphones. If your phone has a country lock, please contact your carrier to remove it before arriving in Korea. Phones from the USA, Canada, Japan, or the Philippines must be fully unlocked to use this SIM.

? How much do outgoing calls and SMS cost? +

Outgoing call and SMS rates depend on your SIM type. An optional voice top-up can be purchased at the airport counter.

? What should I do if the internet doesn't connect? +

If you experience a network connection issue, try resetting your network settings:

  • iPhone: Settings → General → Transfer or Reset iPhone → Reset → Reset Network Settings
  • Android: Settings → General Management → Reset → Reset Network Settings

If the problem persists, contact customer service at 010-3998-1004. Foreigners residing in Korea may also call 114 (free for U+ users) or 080-851-1004 (free for non-U+ users).
